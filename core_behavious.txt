App behavior & validation rules (recommended)
Mandatory fields: patient_id, clinician_id, date_time, consent.signed (true for treatment), working_diagnosis (or reason for visit).

Validation: dates ISO 8601; numeric ranges for vitals and scales; enums for controlled fields.

PHI minimization: do not include sensitive identifiers in filenames; store files in secure storage and reference by document_id.

Audit: log every read/update of patient records.

Retention: follow clinic policy; keep consent and audit logs beyond clinical retention period.

Access: role‑based access; patients can view their own summary but not raw clinician notes unless consented.

Localization: support regional languages and date formats for Indian users.
